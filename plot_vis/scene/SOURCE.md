# Zara1 scene assets

- `crowds_zara01.avi`: UCY "Crowds-by-Example" video (Lerner, Chrysanthou, Lischinski, 2007), 720x576, 25 fps.
  Original host http://graphics.cs.ucy.ac.cy/files/crowds/data/ was unreachable; downloaded from the mirror linked in
  https://github.com/cwang-nus/ETH-UCY-datasets (Google Drive `videos.zip`).
- `crowds_zara01_frame0340.png` / `crowds_zara01_frame0410.png`: video frames 340 and 410, the first and last observed steps of the plotted Zara1 sample
  (annotated pedestrians 12 and 13, frames 340-530 in `datasets/zara1/test/crowds_zara01.txt`).
- `crowds_zara01_image.png`, `crowds_zara01_H.txt`: scene reference image and image-to-world homography from
  SingularTrajectory (`datasets/image`, `datasets/homography`).
